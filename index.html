HIMALAYAN BOTANICAL HERITAGE · INDIA
01 · CHARAKA SAMHITA

Medicine & Rasayana

A foundational Ayurvedic medical compendium. It includes major clinical topics as well as Rasayana and Vajikarana within the wider Ayurvedic framework.

02 · SUSHRUTA SAMHITA

Surgery & Anatomy

A foundational classical text best known for surgery, anatomy and therapeutics, and an important part of the classical Ayurvedic canon.

03 · ASHTANGA HRIDAYA

The Eight Branches

Vagbhata's synthesis of Ayurveda, organised across six sections and covering the eight branches, including Jara and Vrshya traditions.

04 · SHARANGADHARA

The Pharmacy Logic

A later practical text especially important for preparation and pharmaceutics — including powders, decoctions, medicated oils and other dosage forms.

These texts are presented as historical/classical authorities of Ayurveda, not as proof that a modern product automatically reproduces every classical indication or outcome.

TRADITION

Prepared with intention.

Ayurveda is not only about ingredients. Classical texts describe preparation, dosage form, timing, diet, conduct and individual context as part of the larger system.

MODERN EVIDENCE · ED

A 2021 systematic review identified 42 placebo-controlled trials across alternative and herbal approaches to ED. Evidence was heterogeneous; some botanicals showed promising signals, but the review also highlighted limited evidence and the need for better trials.

MODERN EVIDENCE · CHYAWANPRASH

Chyawanprash has a long traditional history, but a critical review notes that human clinical-trial evidence remains limited compared with its widespread traditional use.
