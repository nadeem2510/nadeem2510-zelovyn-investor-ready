CLASSICAL AUTHORITY

Centuries-old knowledge. A modern presentation.

Ayurveda has a long written tradition. This section gives the investor a deeper look into the classical texts and selected botanicals that inform the heritage story. The final commercial ingredient list must always match the approved ZELOVYN formulation.

01

Charaka Samhita

Foundational compendium strongly associated with internal medicine, prevention and Rasayana traditions.

02

Sushruta Samhita

Classical text associated with surgery, anatomy and systematic medical knowledge.

03

Ashtanga Hridaya

A concise synthesis associated with Vagbhata and the eight branches of Ayurveda.

04

Sharangadhara Samhita

Later Ayurvedic compendium with strong emphasis on formulation methods and pharmaceutics.
