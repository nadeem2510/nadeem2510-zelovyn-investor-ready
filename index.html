HIMALAYAN BOTANICAL HERITAGE

From mountain tradition to a modern presentation.

Ayurveda is a knowledge system with a long written tradition. This section introduces the classical literature and selected botanicals relevant to the brand story, while the final commercial ingredient panel should always match the approved ZELOVYN formulation and regulatory documentation.

01

Charaka Samhita

Foundational Ayurvedic compendium with major emphasis on internal medicine, prevention and Rasayana traditions.

02

Sushruta Samhita

Classical text strongly associated with surgery, anatomy and systematic medical knowledge.

03

Ashtanga Hridaya

A concise synthesis associated with Vagbhata and the eight branches of Ayurveda.

04

Sharangadhara Samhita

Important later compendium with strong emphasis on formulation methods and Ayurvedic pharmaceutics.

EVIDENCE, HERITAGE & HONESTY

Ayurvedic heritage and modern evidence are presented separately here on purpose. Reviews of herbal approaches to erectile dysfunction report a growing but heterogeneous evidence base, while recent meta-analysis suggests some herbal interventions may improve erectile-function outcomes but also highlights that evidence differs substantially by ingredient and study quality.

Scientific context: Leisegang & Finelli systematic review of herbal approaches to ED (2021) and Ho et al. systematic review/meta-analysis of herbal dietary supplements for ED (2025).
